# Surface brief: chariscreationsltd.com (all public pages)

Mode: Experience on Home and Projects (the work leads); Persuade on Services and Artistry (choose and book).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The work is the hero. A documentary studio that disappears into moments, so the site recedes into black and photographs carry it. Refuses the agency-template hero with a showreel button.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black planes (#0A0A0A, #1A1A1A), Barlow Condensed 800 uppercase display, Barlow body, hairline rules, sharp rectangles, one hot orange (#F16623) reserved for the primary action.
STORY: Couples and families see real Ugandan ceremonies, trust the studio, then check their date or book on WhatsApp. Organisations follow the same path to a brief.
FIRST VIEWPORT (home): Full-height wall of 18 grayscale photos in 3 drifting rows under a 55% scrim; pinned eyebrow, two-line headline, support line, Check Your Date (primary) and See Our Work.
FORM: Client-pinned. The handover PDF (10 Oct 2026) and the approved hero fixed the direction, so no concept roll was run and there is no seed key.
SIGNATURE MOTION: "Develop": photos enter grayscale and resolve to colour as they scroll in; quiet fade-up reveals. All off under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
