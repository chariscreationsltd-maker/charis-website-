Our Origin Story

BORN FROM
PASSION.
BUILT ON
PURPOSE.

Charis Creations began in 2013 when founder Tumusiime Hudson picked up a camera at a friend's introduction ceremony in Kampala and realized that Uganda's stories weren't being told the way they deserved. That single day changed everything.

What started as a one-man operation filming kukyala ceremonies and weddings grew into a full-service creative studio with a team of 14 passionate storytellers — cinematographers, photographers, editors, drone pilots, and directors — all united by one belief: every frame matters.

Today, Charis Creations is Uganda's most trusted name in cinematic storytelling, having captured over 300 events, portraits, documentaries, and commercial campaigns.
