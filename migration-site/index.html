What We Create

EVERY STORY
WE TELL.

From intimate Kukyala ceremonies to full wedding films, corporate events, and podcasts — we bring cinematic precision to every story worth telling.

01
WEDDING CINEMATOGRAPHY

Your wedding day told as a film. Three cinematographers, aerial drone, and a same-day highlight reel screened at your reception. Every tear, laugh, and dance captured without disturbance.

View Wedding Gallery
02
INTRODUCTION CEREMONIES

The richness of Ugandan Introduction ceremonies captured with the reverence they deserve. From morning preparation to the final celebration — every cultural moment preserved on film.

View Introduction Gallery
03
KUKYALA

Intimate, joyful, full of colour. Rich traditional attire, genuine family moments, and the cinematic storytelling this ceremony deserves — documented with warmth and precision.

View Kukyala Gallery
04
DOCUMENTARIES & SHORT FILMS

Stories that need to be told. Personal profiles, community narratives, brand origin films — documentary content crafted with purpose, depth, and cinematic polish.

View Documentary Work
05
CORPORATE EVENTS

Product launches, award nights, conferences and galas — we bring cinematic discipline to every corporate event, delivering content that elevates your brand long after the event ends.

View Corporate Gallery
06
PODCAST PRODUCTION

Multi-camera production, professional lighting, crisp audio, cinematic colour grading. We make your conversations look as good as they sound — ready for YouTube and beyond.

View Podcast Work

Our Studio

THE STUDIO
THAT GREW
INTO AN AGENCY.

We started 13 years ago filming weddings in Kampala. We were good at it. Then our wedding clients started asking: "Can you do this for our business too?" Then NGOs asked. Then the Office of the President called. So we grew.

Today Charis is a full creative agency — 10+ creatives, a fully equipped studio in Namugongo, and a WhatsApp response time that’s faster than most people’s.Trusted by the Office of the President of Uganda, Compassion International, Coke Studio, and 300+ families across East Africa.

"Unscripted. Unfiltered. Unforgettable."

Trusted By

Office of the President of Uganda · Compassion International · Coke Studio · Save Street Children Uganda · Raise the Roof · Directorate of Ethics & Integrity

And 300+ families who trusted us with their most important days.
